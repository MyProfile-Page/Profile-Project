Maya Enterprise

Items Management System

This online Items management system stores all items of a company in cloud data base. It provides a tabular view of all items of the company alongwith their spot total, Tax & net values. New items can be added and old items can be updated/deleted from items database. Invoice printing facility provides a hard copy of all current items of the company with their spot total values.
