Welcome To Items Catalog System

Create and manage, your items on a cloud database through entering few basic item details such as Category, Manufacturer, supplier, Number, Name, Unit, Price, Quantity, Reorder level, Expiry Date, etc. of your company's items. The system displays on screen all catalog items of the company in a table with a facility to add or update or delete items right in the the display table itself. Additionally a hard copy of the all catalog items can also be printed. The system is available on all medias such as laptop, desktop and mobile phones and needs just an internet connection.
